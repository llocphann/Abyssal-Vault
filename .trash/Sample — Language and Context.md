# Language and Context

## Cue / Questions

- What is context?
- Why can the same sentence mean different things?
- What is the difference between semantics and pragmatics?

## Notes

- **Semantics** studies conventional meaning encoded by linguistic expressions.
- **Pragmatics** studies how speakers and listeners use context to interpret meaning.
- Context can include the physical situation, prior discourse, shared knowledge, and speaker intention.
- A useful test is to ask what information a listener needs beyond the literal sentence to recover the intended message.

## Summary

Meaning is not produced by words alone. Linguistic form contributes conventional meaning, while context helps listeners infer what a speaker intends in a particular situation.
