# Meaning Depends on Context

## Core Idea

The meaning understood in communication is produced by both linguistic form and contextual inference.

## Content

A sentence supplies conventional semantic information, but listeners often need situational knowledge, prior discourse, and assumptions about speaker intent to interpret what was meant. This explains why the same wording can function as a request, warning, joke, or literal statement in different situations.
