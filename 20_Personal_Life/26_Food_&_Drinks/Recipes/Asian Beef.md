ingredients:
  - 1/4 cup Soy sauce
  - 2 teaspoons Cornstarch
  - 1 teaspoon Sugar
  - 1/2 teaspoon Ginger
  - 1 pound Flank steak, trimmed cut in 2" x ⅛" strips
  - 3 tablespoons Vegetable oil
  - 1 medium Green pepper cut into thin strips
  - 20 ounces Pineapple chunks, drained
  - 3 cups Cooked rice
instructions:
  - Mix soy sauce, cornstarch, sugar, and ginger. Coat meat with soy sauce mixture.
  - Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large frypan.
  - Add green pepper strips. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring constantly.
  - Remove green pepper from pan.
  - Heat remaining 2 tablespoons oil.
  - Add meat. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring constantly, until beef is lightly browned.
  - Add green pepper and pineapple.
  - Heat through.
  - Serve over rice.
